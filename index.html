PILLAR 1Understand

What Retention Radar actually does, and whether your studio has what it needs to run it.

🎯 The problem it solves

Most retention reports only catch members with zero visits. They miss the member doing 2 visits a month instead of her usual 8 — her total still looks "okay," so nothing flags her. By the time she cancels, the decline has been going on for months. Retention Radar compares every member against their own baseline, not a studio-wide threshold.

📋 What it produces

A single HTML report your coordinator opens on their phone every Monday: who's urgent (call today), who's sliding (call this week), who's just worth watching, who's new, and who's back from a break — each with a phone number, a script, and a tap-to-call button.

🔒 Data & security, in one place

Retention Radar touches member data in two places. The Google Sheets links (Reports 3, 6, 9, and 10 if Milestone Watch is on) use "Anyone with the link can view" sharing — no login needed to open them, so treat these links like a password: send them only in your weekly email, never post them anywhere public, and delete last week's files before uploading new ones (already part of the routine). Your weekly HTML report — the one with names, phone numbers, and revenue on it — should be hosted on a paid, password-protected Tiiny.host plan, not the free tier, since the free tier's link is the only thing standing between that report and anyone who gets hold of it. Your Claude project only holds your studio's settings and whatever you paste into it each week — nothing is stored anywhere beyond that. None of this makes the system unhackable, but it closes the realistic gap: accidental exposure through forwarding, public posting, or an unauthenticated free link.

PILLAR 2Set up

One-time configuration. Do this once, and every Monday after takes about two minutes on your end.

Confirm your Mindbody reports

In Mindbody, check that you can run: Revenue & Sales, Active Member Count, Membership Cancellations, Suspended Members, Client Contact Details, and Client Health Report (sometimes called the Red/Orange report). Most studios find these under Reports → Clients or Reports → Sales, but Mindbody's menu varies a little by plan — if one isn't where you expect, use Mindbody's report search bar and type the report name directly. One thing worth double-checking: Mindbody has both a membership-level cancellations report and a class-booking cancellations report — you want the one showing member names and dates, not class times. If you're turning on Milestone Watch, you'll also need the First Visit Report — see the note in Step 3 below.

Map your membership tiers

Open your Client Health Report and look at the Membership column. Write down each exact membership name — Retention Radar matches on the exact text, so "Month to Month Membership" and "Month-to-Month Membership" need to be entered exactly as Mindbody shows them. You'll paste these into the Setup Sheet below.

Fill in the Studio Setup Sheet

Six short steps — studio identity, membership tiers, flag sensitivity, outreach tone, and which sections to show (including optional Milestone Watch — lifetime visit milestone celebrations, which adds one more report to your Monday routine if you turn it on). This is also where you choose exactly which situations get escalated to the owner — missing phones, exit interviews running overdue, cancellation spikes, and more — rather than one blanket on/off switch. You can also upload your logo and pick a brand accent colour to personalise the header — purely cosmetic, it never touches the urgent/sliding/watch colour system your coordinator relies on. It generates a config block at the end. It's embedded right below, or open it full-screen ↗.

Create your Google Drive folder

Make one folder where your report runner will upload and convert the Active Members, Suspended Members, and Client Health CSVs into Google Sheets each Monday.

Worth knowing: sharing these as "Anyone with the link can view" means the link itself is what opens the file — same as any quick-share link. Send it in this week's email only, and clear out last week's files before uploading new ones (part of the routine below).

Create your Claude project

Start a new project in Claude.ai. Paste everything the Setup Sheet generates for you — build logic and your studio's settings together — in as the project instructions. That's the entire setup, nothing else to add. The downloaded Guide below is separate: hand that to your report runner, not to Claude.

Set up Tiiny.host — paid plan recommended

Create an account at tiiny.host and turn on password protection (available on any paid plan). This is where your weekly report gets uploaded so your coordinator can open it from a bookmark on their phone — the same link stays live every time you re-upload.

🔒 Why a paid plan, not free

Tiiny's free tier gives you an HTTPS link, but that link is the only barrier — anyone who gets it can open the report, no login required. Your weekly report has real member names, phone numbers, and revenue figures on it, so an unlisted link isn't enough. Any paid Tiiny plan adds password protection, which is the real security floor for this report. Set a password with at least 12 characters and share it with your coordinator separately from the link itself (e.g. a text, not the same email).

PILLAR 3Run it

What actually happens every Monday, once setup is done.

Report runner exports the reports (~30 min)

No filters applied to any report — filtered data causes missed at-risk members and wrong revenue figures. Two are sent as CSV attachments, four (or five, with Milestone Watch) as Google Sheet links, in one email.

Paste the links into Claude (~2 min)

In your Claude project: "Run this week's report — here are the links: [4 links]", then drop in the 2 CSV files.

Claude verifies, then builds

Every file is checked before anything is built — right columns present, right sharing permissions, right file for the right report. If something fails, Claude tells you exactly what to fix and re-send. If everything passes, it builds the HTML report.

Upload to Tiiny.host (~1 min)

Upload the new HTML file. Your coordinator's saved link and password keep working — no new URL or password to share each week.

Coordinator works the list

Starts with the red (urgent) cards, works down through amber and blue over the week. Tap-to-call, then tick Contacted / Cancelled / On hold — ticks are saved on that device automatically, so nothing resets if they close the tab and come back tomorrow.

Common hiccups & how to fix them

Revenue looks too low
The revenue file was probably exported with a filter still on. Fix: re-run the report with every filter cleared — memberships, packs, drop-ins, fees, and online store all need to show up together.
Active member count looks wrong
Check the first column of the file — it should show membership group names. If it says "Sale Date" instead, the revenue report was sent by mistake. Fix: re-export the Active Member Count report specifically.
A Google Sheet link won't open
The sheet is probably still set to restricted access. Fix: in Google Drive, set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view" before sending the link.
A member card is missing a phone number
Their mobile number isn't in Mindbody, or the Client Contact Details export didn't include the phone column. Fix: re-export with the mobile field included, and update the member's card in Mindbody if it's genuinely missing.
Client Contact Details report is locked / permission error
Some Mindbody staff logins don't have access to client-data reports. Fix: use the Mailing List report instead (Reports → Clients → Mailing Lists), generated for all clients — not filtered to marketing opt-ins only, or unsubscribed members will be missing from the file and their cards will look like they've left the studio.
Someone on a 2x/week membership got flagged as barely attending
Two visits on a 2x/week cap is full attendance, not decline. Fix: check your Setup Sheet threshold for that tier — it should be one less than the weekly cap.
Milestone Watch is missing members or showing the wrong count (Milestone Watch studios only)
Report 10 (First Visit Report) was probably run as a "TODAY snapshot" like the other reports. Fix: re-run it with the date range set to your studio's actual opening date through to today — every week, not just the first time.
Cancelled section is empty or showing class times instead of member dates
Mindbody has two different "cancellations" reports, and it's easy to grab the wrong one. Fix: re-export the membership-level Cancellations report (Report 5) — it should show member names with "Member From"/"Member To" dates, not class times.

🔧 Updating your settings as your studio changes

Prices change, tiers get renamed, teams change — here's the simplest way to handle each, without waiting on a full rebuild:

Price, threshold, or team-name change: open your Claude project's instructions, find the line for that value, and edit it directly. It's just text — no need to touch the Setup Sheet for a small change like this.

Adding or removing a membership tier, or turning a section like Milestone Watch on or off: re-open the Setup Sheet, make the change there, and paste the newly generated config back into your Claude project in place of what's there now. This is exactly what the Setup Sheet is built to handle.

Either way, your coordinator's tick history stays exactly as it was — it's saved on their device, not inside the config, so updating your settings never resets it. If you ever want something the report doesn't do at all yet (a new field, section, or report type), that's a build request rather than a settings change.

⬡ Rules that never change

  • 14+ days with no visit is always urgent, regardless of any other number
  • Staff/comp memberships are always excluded from every count and every flag
  • A membership's renewal date is a billing date, never a sign someone is about to cancel
  • Every file is checked before a report gets built — if a check fails, nothing gets built until it's fixed
