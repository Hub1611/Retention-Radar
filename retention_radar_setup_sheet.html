💡 Tier types: 1 = Unlimited (no cap on classes), 2 = Mid-tier (e.g. 3-4x/week), 3 = Lite (capped at 2x/week or similar), 4 = Class pack (non-recurring). The system uses tiers to set appropriate thresholds and scripts.
💡 If you sell class packs, add them here for labelling only (tier type 4) — their actual "who needs a call" logic is handled separately by the Pack Holders section (Step 5: Sections), not by the weekly cap/threshold columns, since a pack doesn't have a weekly cadence expectation the way a recurring membership does.
💡 Threshold = visits/30 days below which a member is flagged as "barely attending". Enter a weekly cap and this fills in automatically at (cap − 1) — so a 2x/week cap auto-fills threshold 1, because 2 visits = maxing cap = healthy. You can still type over the auto-filled number if you want a different threshold. This isn't a one-time setting — come back and adjust it anytime your business needs change; just regenerate and re-paste your project instructions afterward.

Flag Sensitivity 🔄 Adjust anytime
Control how sensitive the system is at catching at-risk members. The defaults are proven — only adjust if you find you're getting too many or too few flags.
💡 The ghost threshold (14 days absent = urgent) is locked and never changes. It is the single most reliable early warning signal in member retention research.
💡 Why -30% to -60% for Sliding? This is the tested range. Below -30%, Sliding starts overlapping with the Watch threshold (default -20%) — "call this week" and "just keep an eye on them" stop being distinct signals, and you get noise instead of a clean escalation path. Above -60%, a member has to decline a long way before you'd hear about it — often too late to be an early warning. -40% is the tested default. If your studio's own data says a different number works better for you, you can still set a custom value below — this range is a recommendation, not a hard limit.
-40%
Member's visits drop X% vs their own 6-month average → amber sliding card. Lower = more sensitive (more cards). Higher = less noise. Default: -40%.
% decline vs personal average
-20%
Member's visits drop X% vs their own average → blue watch card (not yet urgent). Default: -20%.
14d
Days absent before a member is flagged as urgent ghost. Locked at 14 days — this is the proven retention science threshold.
+10%
Member's visits are up X% or more (but below the Thriving threshold) vs their own average → lighter-touch climbing card. Default: +10%.
+20%
Member's visits are up X% or more vs their own average → standout thriving card. Mirrors Sliding, just in the opposite direction. Default: +20%.
10/mo
Alternate path into Thriving: for uncapped tiers, visits this month at or above this number (and not declining) also counts as thriving — even with no percentage jump. Default: 10/month (~2-3x/week). For capped tiers (like a 2x/week Lite membership), hitting the cap itself always counts — no separate number needed.
💡 Why three paths? Not every member worth recognizing looks the same. Climbing catches a modest, real uptick (+10-20%) that deserves a nod without the full fanfare. Thriving catches the standout cases — either a big jump (+20% or more) or a member who's been consistently excellent for a long time and would otherwise never be recognized simply because their number never moves. All three are about the same thing: members feeling seen by the business — not just a referral tactic.
